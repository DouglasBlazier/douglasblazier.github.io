🛡️Built-in safety checks

Automation is only useful if it can be trusted. Every skill follows the same ground rules, so a fast mistake never replaces a slow, correct job.

Numbers must match

Before anything is keyed, totals are checked against the source document: the credit register, the count sheet or the vendor confirmation. If they don't match, the job stops.

ExampleRegister total 42 cases = sheet total 42 cases ✓

Asks instead of guessing

Hard-to-read handwriting, an item that isn't in the system or anything outside the rules becomes a question for me, never a guess.

Example"Row 7 could be 18 or 13. Which is it?"

I review the results

Results come back as a short summary or a marked-up PDF that I check. Nothing goes to a vendor or a coworker on its own.

ExamplePO 230301: 2 price differences marked for review

Every run is logged

Each job records what it read, what it keyed and the result, so any number can be traced back to where it came from.

Example9:36 AM · NT · 4 posted · 1 skipped · totals match
